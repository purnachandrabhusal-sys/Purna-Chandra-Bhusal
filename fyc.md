## Open-access resources I recommend

<!-- These are real, free, well-established resources in writing
     studies. Keep the ones you actually use and delete the rest —
     a curated list is more credible than a comprehensive one, and
     what you choose tells readers something about how you teach. -->

- [The WAC Clearinghouse](https://wacclearinghouse.org/) — open-access books, journals, and teaching materials in writing studies.
- [Writing Spaces: Readings on Writing](https://writingspaces.org/) — peer-reviewed open textbook essays written for undergraduate writers, free to assign.
- [Composition Forum](https://compositionforum.com/) — open-access journal on composition theory and pedagogy.
- [Kairos](https://kairos.technorhetoric.net/) — open-access journal of rhetoric, technology, and pedagogy.
- [The Council of Writing Program Administrators](https://wpacouncil.org/) — the WPA Outcomes Statement and related position statements.
